## Readiness summary

- **No candidate is cleared as a direct external validation of the existing pasture behavior baseline.** The Chile source has the closest labels and setting but lacks an explicit data license in the repository. XGain provides only classifier-derived summaries and has conflicting license statements. Precision Beef is licensed and group-identifiable, but it measures housed feeding behavior under a different ontology and production system.
- Precision Beef supports a separately scoped 90-second collar-to-instrument agreement diagnostic. Its 18 animals and timestamp-window coverage do not make it a grazing validation set or human-confirmed ground truth.
- XGain's open ZIP was inspected and normalized, but it cannot validate a classifier without independent reference labels and is excluded from commercial training pending license clarification.
- The Chile CSVs are technically accessible at modest size, but the user-set rule requiring explicit reuse permission takes precedence. No researcher contact or permission request was made.
- These are behavior-only resources. None provides DMI, ADG, or independent validation of the Riose scientific motor.
