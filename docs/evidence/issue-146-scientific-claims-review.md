## Calibration, validation, assumptions, limitations

- **Calibrated components:** none of the farm simulator's biological or environmental parameters are calibrated to a target farm/population in the reviewed repository.
- **Validation:** no full-engine biological validation or farm-specific predictive-accuracy evidence was found. The BR-CORTE equation has published independent validation in its stated domain; it is a separate, limited component and requires an entered ADG. Existing engine ADG/DMI checks are explicitly labeled component diagnostics, transfer checks, or no-fit comparisons, not validation or calibration.
- **Main limitations:** illustrative default intake, maintenance, gain, forage-growth, weather, cost, and thirst rules; no measured farm inputs; no real water balance; no area/species/quality model for pasture; no validated ration formulation or feed-access/capacity model; no calibration or predictive-uncertainty estimates; no causal evidence for scenario differences.
- **Scientific sources and status:** `docs/modelos/br-corte-2023-cms-pasto.md`, `docs/evidence/scientific-module-evaluations-2026-10-13.md`, `docs/evidence/dmi-adg-readiness-v1.md`, the engine component diagnostic JSON reports, `src/client/engine.js`, `src/client/environment-model.js`, and `src/client/thermal-index.js`.
