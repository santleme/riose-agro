# Riose Product V4 constitution

Product V4 adds decision-intelligence workflows over registered, bounded calculations. It extends `specs/riose-product-v3/` and `specs/riose-product-v2/`; neither replaces their contracts.

## Rules

- Scientific outputs must come from a versioned model, declared dataset, or transparent arithmetic. No LLM may produce numeric scientific results.
- The BR-CORTE 2023 Eq. 2.6 calculation is conditional on user-supplied ADG and published input domain; it does not predict ADG/gain. Preserve source-reported external validation versus absence of Riose-specific calibration.
- FarmEngine stays demonstrative and separate from registered scientific results. The Perea semi-Markov candidate stays NO-GO and disconnected.
- Require review and explicit confirmation before executable work. Edits invalidate review and never execute automatically.
- Keep same-seed/snapshot paired comparisons and historical contracts compatible. Version breaking changes.
- No paid provider call without the existing provider-spend gate. No external contacts without authorization.
- Passing software tests is not biological validation or commercial approval.
- Prefer small vanilla JS/HTML/CSS/Worker changes, TDD, measured performance work, and progressive disclosure.

## Task status

Use `BACKLOG`, `READY`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `BLOCKED`, `VALIDATING`, `DONE`. `DONE` requires the task's acceptance evidence and passing relevant checks. See `RUN_LOOP.md` and `tasks.md`.
