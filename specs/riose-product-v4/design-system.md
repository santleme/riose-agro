# V4 design system

Extend `specs/riose-product-v3/06-design-system.md` and `specs/riose-product-v2/06-design-system.md`: off-white canvas, precise system typography, tabular numeric values, low-contrast surfaces, restrained blue, short functional transitions, visible keyboard focus and `prefers-reduced-motion`.

The decision workspace should progressively disclose conversation, experiment comparison, and contextual provenance. On small screens use a single-column flow; do not keep three dense panels open. Every chart names its model, unit, domain and interpretation boundary. Empty forms must not imply observations: keep scientific inputs blank until entered or explicitly load a labeled example. Never animate fabricated values.
