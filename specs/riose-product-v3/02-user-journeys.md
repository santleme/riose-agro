# User journeys

## Compare scenarios

1. Open Product and select **Illustrative scenarios** or a named scientific calculator.
2. Enter inputs and see which are observed/user-supplied, assumptions, or unavailable.
3. Configure one baseline and at least two alternatives; only the declared factor may differ in a FarmEngine set.
4. Review common snapshot, seed, factor, horizon, executor/model and limits.
5. Explicitly run. Show progress and an explicit cancel action for long runs.
6. Inspect named outputs, deltas, sensitivity values and population observations where the selected executor/model supplies them.
7. Save/replay/export the complete versioned manifest and provenance.

## BR-CORTE DMI calculator

1. Choose the published BR-CORTE 2023 Eq. 2.6 calculator.
2. Provide BW, **assumed ADG**, supplement DM, population count and horizon. Require an explicit distribution or keep a representative-animal calculation; never fabricate individual weights.
3. Optionally provide supplement price per kg DM and currency. Without this, show no cost.
4. Review domain/ranges, model and equation source. Reject unsupported populations/conditions and out-of-domain inputs or label any extension separately as unavailable.
5. Compare three or more supplement levels. Show total DMI estimate and transparent arithmetic rollups. Do not show predicted gain, physiological response, forage stock, or heat effects.

## AI research assistance

The assistant may draft a scenario plan, retrieve registered model limits and summarize actual completed results. It cannot produce numbers, modify formulas, choose hidden defaults, or execute until the user confirms the validated plan. Provider absence leaves deterministic local guidance available.
