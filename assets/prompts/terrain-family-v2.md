# Terrain family V2 prompts

## Dirt path transition overlay

Create one transparent PNG of a narrow 2:1 isometric farm road ribbon in the same pixel-art style, palette, scale, lighting and cluster density as `pasture.grass.premium.imagegen.v1`. Use compacted muted umber/sienna earth, broad calm tonal patches and very subtle wear. The edge must be irregular and interwoven: a few grass clumps intrude into the track, with a modest dust/soil fringe. No opaque diamond tile, hard slab edge, flowers, dense noise, photorealism, 3D, text, or background. The PNG is a compositing overlay with true transparency outside the road.

## Compacted ground patch

Create one transparent PNG of an asymmetric low 2:1 isometric patch of compacted/worn earth for grounding a farmhouse entrance, tractor, cattle gathering point or corral. Match the same Riose pasture sprite's pixel language, olive/sage/umber palette, outline delicacy, lighting and scale. Brown earth occupies the center and blends through flattened grass around an irregular perimeter. Keep details broad and calm, with minimal hoof/tire wear and a few vegetation interruptions. No hard diamond boundary, decorative flowers, noise, text, background, photorealism or 3D.
