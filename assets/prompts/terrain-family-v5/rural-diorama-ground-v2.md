# Ground plate V2 — generation brief

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone isometric terrain plate for the Riose browser farm simulator, under existing image sprites.
Primary request: Generate one complete Brazilian cattle-farm diorama ground plate with clean alpha transparency outside the plate. Make it a broad 2:1 isometric diamond with visible, thin, dark red-brown earth thickness on the front-facing edges, like a polished miniature map slab. The surface should read as one designed territory: controlled green pasture, one compact cultivated furrow parcel, a winding compacted dirt access road that connects the zones, and subtle worn earth around the route. Blend grass and dirt with irregular, soft transitions. Use broad calm color masses, a few intentional grass clusters, sparse flowers, low texture noise, and quiet breathing space so buildings, cattle, trees, fences and machines can be placed on top later.

## Reference roles

- Urban master: art finish, pixel density, thin outlines, shading, diorama presentation only; do not copy objects or city layout.
- Fazenda V7: rural content scale and density only; do not copy exact layout or buildings.
- Riose V2 ranch scene: terrain color, grass-to-dirt transitions, localized use and grounding only.

## Style and constraints

Refined 2D isometric game pixel art; crisp deliberate clusters and stepped pixel edges, fine dark outlines, restrained moderately desaturated palette, 2:1 game projection, graphic shading in a few tones. Consistent with existing Riose sprite art. Broad landscape plate, full diamond visible with margin. Keep pasture dominant; crop and dirt areas secondary and integrated. Consistent soft upper-left game light. Terrain-only surface: no animals, buildings, fences, trees, equipment, water, labels, text, UI, inset panels, separate tiles, grid, or contact shadows from absent props. No literal copying of reference compositions. No generic cartoon, painterly concept art, 3D render, soft airbrush gradients, excess micro-noise, or scattered flowers. Request true transparent alpha outside the diamond.
